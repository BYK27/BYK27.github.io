CH 33

PupilPrism

Master's thesis, 2026

AndroidKotlinJetpack ComposeRoom

An RSVP speed reader. It shows text one word at a time and changes how long each word stays on screen based on its length and punctuation. The pivot letter is highlighted so your eyes don't have to move.

It imports PDFs and web articles, finds your reading speed with a comprehension test, tracks words read and streaks, and generates PDF reports. I tested it in a user study with 9 participants against reading on paper.
